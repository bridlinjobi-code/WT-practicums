Welcome to Smart Agriculture

Smart agriculture uses Internet of Things (IoT) sensors, connected controllers, and cloud software to help farmers understand their land and crops in real time. By measuring soil conditions, automating irrigation, and watching crop health, farmers can save water, reduce losses, and grow more with less effort.

Our Smart Agriculture Solutions

Explore how IoT technology helps farmers manage soil, water, and crops.

Soil Monitoring

Soil sensors placed in the field measure moisture, temperature, pH, and nutrient levels, so farmers know exactly when and where the soil needs attention.

  • Soil moisture sensors
  • Soil pH and nutrient sensors
  • Soil temperature tracking
  • Alerts for dry or poor soil

Smart Irrigation

Smart irrigation links soil data to pumps and valves, delivering water only when and where crops need it. Farmers can control the system from a phone.

  • Automatic pump and valve control
  • Drip and sprinkler scheduling
  • Weather-based watering
  • Water usage reports

Crop Monitoring

Crop monitoring combines field sensors, weather stations, and camera images to track plant growth and spot pests, disease, or heat stress early.

  • Plant growth tracking
  • Pest and disease alerts
  • Weather and humidity monitoring
  • Drone and camera imaging

How Smart Farming Works

Field sensors collect information about soil and crops. The data travels through a network to a cloud platform, where it is analysed and shown to the farmer on a webpage or mobile app.

  1. Field sensors: Measure soil moisture, temperature, and crop conditions.
  2. Connectivity: Send readings using LoRa, GSM, or Wi-Fi networks.
  3. Data processing: Cloud software analyses soil and crop data.
  4. User interface: A webpage or mobile app shows readings and alerts to the farmer.
  5. Action: Farmers or automated irrigation systems respond to the data.

Key Smart Agriculture Components

Sensors and devices used on a smart farm
Component Purpose Example
Soil moisture sensor Measures water content in the soil Capacitive moisture probe
Controller Processes sensor data and switches pumps and valves Arduino or ESP32 microcontroller
Network Sends field data to the cloud LoRa, GSM, or Wi-Fi
Cloud platform Stores and analyses farm data Cloud data service
Application Shows readings and lets farmers control irrigation Farmer mobile app
